Zoomer is the orange tabby who treats this neighborhood like a personal resort. Mornings are for warm car roofs. Afternoons are for the bakery stoop, where the smell of butter does half the convincing. Evenings are for a slow fence-line patrol, tail up, checking that everyone got home.

If you see a cat with a collar tag and the confident walk of someone who pays no rent, that's Zoomer. Say hello if he allows it. Then tell the map where you crossed paths.
