Suggested tag

A printed epoxy pet tag holds a color QR well: an aluminum or stainless blank with a full-color print under a clear dome. Engraved metal works too if the QR stays high contrast, dark modules on a light ground. The code uses high error correction so a scratch is less likely to break the scan.

45 × 72 mm is roomy for an adult cat collar and too big for a tiny kitten. For a smaller collar, print only the QR at 25 mm on a round tag about 32 mm across and shorten the words to "I'm Zoomer — scan me".

Test the scan in daylight and in a dim hallway. Then attach it.
